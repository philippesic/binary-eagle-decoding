1. **SFDD target-flatness selection — first bounded probe.**

   SFDD ranks complete training samples by average target-distribution flatness. For normalized probabilities, its cosine-to-uniform statistic simplifies to `1 / sqrt(V × sum(p²))`. This requires only one stored scalar per row after streaming exact full-vocabulary logits. Its EAGLE-2 experiments support useful data selection, including greedy evaluation. However, the headline “50% data, within 4% speedup” should not be transferred to our greedy campaign: the greedy appendix reports average speedup **2.80× full-data, 2.62× at 50% retention, and 2.70× at 70%**. The theory uses probability-ratio acceptance and an idealized distribution update; it does not prove improvements under our greedy verifier or binary QAT. [Paper, equations 6/8 and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.18902v1). The author implementation additionally defines a filtering “sample” as an entire batch, so its selection unit needs adaptation. [Author code guide](https://github.com/fjm9933/Flatness).

   **Local hook and cost:** derive scalars beside the authenticated raw-logit/prefix mapping in [prepare_w1ax_capture_shards.py:358](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/scripts/prepare_w1ax_capture_shards.py:358), preserving the join at [line 451](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/scripts/prepare_w1ax_capture_shards.py:451). Select whole rounds through [prepared_continuous_provider.py:188](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/scripts/prepared_continuous_provider.py:188) or whole anchors through [w1ax_capture_provider.py:878](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/scripts/w1ax_capture_provider.py:878). No inference change or extra teacher training is required. Scoring adds one streaming raw-logit pass; storage is small. Existing compact top-k data with a uniform-tail approximation cannot recover exact `sum(p²)`.

   **Hypothesis:** stratified selection could improve acceptance gained per training second by reducing repeated low-information rounds. Counterargument: binary compression may make confident, apparently easy rows difficult; discarding them could damage roots and rare domains.

   **Minimal test:** on one balanced TRAIN pool, compare uniform ordering, random 70% retention, and highest-flatness 70% retention, stratified by domain and context-length bin. Preserve chronology within every retained chain. Use identical initialization and hard-forward precision, evaluating at 100k and 200k presented supervised tokens. Also report the endpoint at common total charged compute, including scoring. Measure unique prompts/rows, supported and unsupported coverage, root agreement, per-position survival, and clean request throughput. Confirm the winner on independent development prompts.

   **Recommendation:** probe; do not discard 30–50% of the final corpus by default. Admit separately for A8/A1 and each family. Block models need their own five-tap, anchor and mask data first.

2. **AdaSPEC residual-learnability filtering — conditional probe using an existing matched reference.**

   A precise correction to common descriptions: AdaSPEC does **not** simply select lowest-loss tokens. It trains a reference of the draft architecture, computes `student_loss − reference_loss`, and retains the **largest** gaps: errors the trained reference demonstrates are learnable. Its top-40% selection beats bottom-40%; its hard-label fine-tuning ablation also improves acceptance. Evidence covers ordinary Pythia/CodeGen drafters, not W1 QAT or DSpark. Training the reference is an additional cost that cannot disappear from accounting. [Paper, equations 7–10, Tables 2–3, Appendix A.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.19779v1).

   **Local hook and cost:** obtain per-row CE from [recurrent_loss.py:17](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/src/w1a1_eagle/recurrent_loss.py:17), replacing the scalar mean at [line 40](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/src/w1a1_eagle/recurrent_loss.py:40) only in a declared experimental objective. Integrate through [recurrent_qat.py:763](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/src/w1a1_eagle/recurrent_qat.py:763). Preserve all recurrent forwards and attached states even where a token receives no loss. Cache reference CE by capture hash, prompt, exact prefix and row identity.

   The inexpensive local adaptation would reuse a **matched all-label binary development checkpoint** as reference and compare hard-label CE gaps. This is a CE-regret adaptation, not an exact reproduction of full-distribution AdaSPEC. Frozen Q4 is a weaker proxy: it demonstrates higher-precision learnability, not binary representability. Do not launch another long reference lineage solely for this screen.

   **Hypothesis:** prioritize recoverable binary mistakes rather than spending capacity on irreducible errors. Counterargument: reference scores can encode that checkpoint’s early domain exposure and initialization; a static mask can suppress later-learnable examples.

   **Minimal test:** after meaningful balanced reference exposure, score a small authenticated TRAIN pool. Compare uniform CE, highest raw-student-CE selection, and highest CE-regret selection at the same selected fraction. Rank across a reasonably sized pool, not within a five-row chain. First use one frozen score manifest; avoid continual rescoring. Report both presented tokens and selected loss-bearing tokens, since masking does not save recurrent forward work. Match training-token rungs and separately total compute including reference scoring and attributable reference training. Reject if early-position survival or any domain deteriorates despite aggregate CE improvement.

   **Recommendation:** conditional probe after an appropriate reference exists. Keep uniform-data long QAT as the default. Do not combine this immediately with SFDD: one selects teacher uncertainty, the other demonstrated student improvability, and their intersection may become narrow.

3. **VAT rejection-anchored weighting — promising precise refinement, with a prefix-contract gate.**

   VAT changes the decay anchor per sample: positions through the first rejection receive full weight; decay begins afterwards. This differs from the existing fixed early-depth weighting. The full method also adds a training-only survival classifier and hard-plus-soft CE. Its main experiments explicitly simulate **greedy** verification, so rejecting it merely because the introductory formula uses ratio acceptance would be incorrect. On A100/BF16/Hugging Face, Qwen3-4B EAGLE-3 acceptance length rises 6.28→6.78 and DFlash 5.73→6.08. These are full-method results, not evidence for weighting alone or native W1 gains. It is a recent preprint. [Paper, sections 4–5 and Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.30135v1).

   **Local hook and limitation:** weighting can extend [qat_curriculum.py:30](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/src/w1a1_eagle/qat_curriculum.py:30), whose reduction is at [line 54](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/src/w1a1_eagle/qat_curriculum.py:54). But [recurrent_rollout.py:173](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/src/w1a1_eagle/recurrent_rollout.py:173) follows **captured proposal tokens**, while recurrent hidden states come from the current student. A current-argmax mismatch scan therefore measures agreement under captured conditioning; it is not automatically the current drafter’s native first rejection.

   Exact current-chain labels require native verification at those actual prefixes. Never replace captured tokens with current predictions while retaining old labels. The existing exact-prefix ancestry check at [recurrent_trace.py:190](/Users/pippo/github/binary-eagle-decoding/src/w1a1_eagle/recurrent_trace.py:190) must remain mandatory.

   **Hypothesis:** adaptable weighting could preserve useful later positions when the student already survives early positions. Counterargument: with weak binary roots, rejection usually occurs immediately, reducing VAT to ordinary depth decay; incorrect survival labels could reinforce the wrong trajectory.

   **Minimal test:** first audit the proposed rejection signal against freshly captured native current-model chains on a bounded balanced TRAIN subset. If valid, compare fixed normalized depth weighting with rejection-anchored weighting alone at common token rungs and compute. Retain nonzero later loss and separate weighted exposure. Only then consider the auxiliary head and soft-label additions.

   **Recommendation:** defer full VAT until the current-prefix label path is admitted. A cheap captured-conditioning weighting screen is permissible if labeled explicitly as a proxy; it cannot establish native survival supervision. DSpark needs separate confirmation because its Markov/confidence objectives differ.

A plausible idea to **reject from the initial recipe** is generic uniform/Gaussian feature-noise augmentation. Original EAGLE used uniform feature noise to address feature autoregression errors. [Original paper](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=1NdN7eXyb4). Here, hard QAT already produces deployment quantization errors and recurrence already feeds current student states. Unmeasured additive noise may duplicate or distort those errors, especially for A1. Reconsider only after measuring an uncovered native-versus-training error component and designing noise to match it.
