# Tiny corrections to a mostly binary drafter

Research audit, 2026-10-01. Source baseline `f0bb92dda687d908b3cffe084510b3be757df54f`. No model execution, fitting, tests, GPU/Metal/remote access, or sealed-final inspection occurred. Q4_0 is the primary comparison; target/verifier precision stays frozen. This report proposes a later calibration screen, not a new experiment authorization.

## Verdict and error location

**Pursue a GPU-free correction-readiness dossier; conditionally pursue one rank-1/4 fusion correction.** Retain output bias as a cheap control and static channel exceptions as a capture-dependent alternative. Defer a general adapter stack, another dense-head rescue, and residual binary planes until the error structure supports them.

The [completed rescue](../binary-rescue-head-5080.md) makes body correction more credible than head expansion. On common forced histories, D body under Q4 head lost 19.8 percentage points of first-position agreement; D head under Q4 body lost 5.9. D accepted 0.425 drafts/round, attention rescue 0.609, fusion rescue 0.533, and their combination 0.696 versus Q4_0's 1.042. The fitted dense head reached 0.555 and 0.450× Q4 request throughput. These are RTX 5080, group128 **W1A16** results, not isolated A1 evidence or an SM75 prediction. The head intervention is not an upper bound on every possible adapter, but substantially weakens a head-first allocation.

For A1, the same-input error decomposes as

`Wx − Wb q(x) = (W−Wb)x + Wb(x−q(x))`.

The first term loses weight magnitudes; the second loses activation directions/magnitudes. Fusion combines three target taps; Q/K/V concatenate separately normalized embedding and state streams; FFN-down sees a SiLU/product distribution. RMSNorm does not remove coordinate means. Fusion error reaches every subsequent state; K/V errors persist in caches; Q errors can alter attention routing rather than merely output amplitude. None of these observations establishes that the residual is low-rank or confined to outliers. The [previous precision proposal](../research-review-2026-09-25/05-precision.md) and [training audit](../training-optimization-audit-2026-10-01.md) correctly require capture-led selection.

## What primary research actually supports

[LoRC, AAAI 2024, Tables 7–9](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/29908/31587) applies SVD to weight quantization residuals. Rank four changes OPT-6.7B W4A16 perplexity from 12.06 to 12.00; rank eight reaches 11.99. Its main residual factors are INT8, with FP16 also tested. The modest W4 gains and larger W2 gains justify a tiny-rank screen, not an assumed binary rescue.

[LQ-LoRA v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.12023v3) alternates quantization and low-rank decomposition, optionally Fisher-weighted. Its principal rank is 64, and 2.75-bit LLaMA-2-70B uses 2.85 effective bits including corrections; WikiText perplexity is 3.65 versus 3.31 uncompressed. Rank/capacity and continual training exceed the proposed rank-1/4 patch. [QLoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14314) establishes efficient adaptation through a frozen 4-bit model, but its NF4/dequantized computation is not binary activation execution.

[LRC v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07902v1) directly addresses activation error by feeding the correction **unquantized activations**. It reports over half the W4A4 accuracy gap recovered with substantial rank budgets, and appendix C.2 notes effective 6.08-bit storage for its chosen setting. Its A100 latency experiment uses batch32×sequence2048 and still incurs overhead; it cannot predict our N=1 correction cost. This is the most relevant conceptual precedent for raw-input correction, with much weaker evidence for tiny rank.

[BiLLM v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.04291v2) selectively uses binary residual approximation on salient weights: LLaMA2-70B achieves WikiText perplexity 8.41 at 1.08 weight bits versus FP16's 3.32; 7B reaches 32.48 versus 5.47. It supports uneven bit allocation, and also illustrates severe remaining distortion. [LLM.int8 v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2208.07339v2) shows critical activation outliers concentrated in roughly 0.1% of feature dimensions in its studied models. That empirical pattern must be demonstrated in this small EAGLE component rather than transferred by analogy. Causal-LM perplexity and downstream accuracy are distinct from native greedy draft matching and accepted-prefix survival.

## Three narrow variants

**1. Output-bias calibration: pursue as a control, conditional deployment.** Add a regularized vocabulary offset `b` to fixed binary logits. A 32,000-entry F32 vector costs 125 KiB and one addition/logit, potentially fused with the existing scaling/store. Unlike a common positive temperature, row-specific bias can change greedy ordering. A same-input MSE offset is simply mean residual; target-aligned CE fitting is a different objective and needs native label joins and shrinkage for rare tokens. Bias cannot restore input information or add unsupported vocabulary tokens. It may learn domain token frequencies and improve CE while worsening context-specific matching. This remains compatible with the project's selected-linear W1A1 definition because normal-precision bias is allowed, although it requires explicit export/graph support and is not the frozen contract.

**2. One fusion rank-1/4 residual: conditional lead.** Execute `Wb q(x) + U(Vᵀx)` using raw pre-quantization input, frozen binary base, F16 factors and declared accumulation/cast order. For fusion's 2560×7680 shape, rank one/four adds 20/80 KiB and 10,240/40,960 MACs per input row. Rank four is 0.21% of dense fusion MACs, but two reductions/products, intermediate storage, input rereads and possibly two launches dominate small-N overhead. A float correction is an honest **binary-core hybrid**, even if its parameter count is tiny. Feeding `q(x)` instead avoids raw magnitude information and cannot distinguish inputs with identical complete quantized representations; compare that as attribution, not as an equivalent implementation.

Fit captured output residuals through regularized reduced-rank regression on real input covariance; plain weight SVD is a weaker baseline. Center separately so a mean offset does not consume rank one. If fusion residual structure is diffuse, consider exactly one attention projection selected by captured output relevance, rather than four independent adapters. A shared rank-four input factor for Q/K/V costs 88 KiB total, but shared bases constrain three different residual subspaces. K/V modifications require current-student cache rebuild and recurrent state replay. Fusion is simpler because its direct input is captured target features, though changing it still changes downstream recurrence.

**3. Eight fixed fusion input-channel exceptions: conditional alternative.** Choose a static set from train-only, prompt-stable error contributions, not activation absmax alone. Replace the corresponding binary contribution with original F16 weights and raw inputs; explicitly subtract the old contribution or exclude those coordinates from the packed base. Eight columns add about 40 KiB of F16 payload plus indices, 20,480 MACs/row, gathers and output accumulation. Raw activations with binary weights repair only activation error; F16 columns repair both terms. Neither is strict all-W1A1. Preserve quantizer scale semantics when removing channels. Isolated sparse weights need index traffic and irregular access; column/block exceptions are more credible than arbitrary COO entries at N=1.

Residual binary planes are an alternative if weight error dominates: store another sign/scale representation of selected residual blocks. A complete extra plane adds nearly another bit/weight and another packed dot; A1 on both planes still loses activation magnitude. Sparse planes require masks/maps and irregular work. Selectively widening whole tiny K/V projections is simpler native engineering, but each is 5.24M weights and can no longer be described as a negligible exception. Defer both alternatives unless the small correction screen fails for a specific measured reason.

## Best first two GPU-free days and later gate

Deliver **one provenance-bound readiness and error-structure dossier**, with frozen budgets for bias, rank1/4 fusion, and eight columns. Day one inventories small local manifests, allowed split IDs, operand availability, casting contracts and loader gaps. Day two specifies streaming residual means, covariance sketches, top singular energy and channel-stability checks, with per-prompt train-only holdouts and exact byte/MAC/launch accounting. Fit only later if separately authorized and locally available; absence of operands is a useful readiness result, not permission to fetch remote captures or run models.

Minimum useful calibration is joined raw inputs and corresponding binary/reference outputs, source weight/quantizer hashes, projection layout, prompt/domain/depth IDs and boundaries. Head bias additionally needs supported native target labels and fixed-head logits or normalized head operands. A bounded example budget is 256 rows from at least 12 training prompts across prose/code/reasoning, split by prompt; this can reject diffuse corrections but cannot establish rare-token calibration or deployment quality. Cached head states cannot identify fusion/attention residuals. Local rescue metadata is present, but its index does not establish local train operands; the full96 and shard0000 reports locate major raw bundles remotely. Small local smoke/stage captures exist, with limited distribution coverage and readiness restrictions. Never silently upgrade preparation-only data to training eligibility.

The decisive counterargument is that A1 error may be broad and input-dependent: tiny rank/bias/outliers could reduce MSE while cache drift and changed proposals erase acceptance. Later, one authorized native 5080 gate should compare the selected candidate, unchanged binary parent and Q4_0 on fixed development prompts, preserving the verifier and checking raw outputs, depth survival, complete rounds and complete-operator latency. Stop if acceptance gains cannot pay correction and extra verification cost. An SM75 throughput claim still requires actual 2080 Ti measurement after its pause is released.

Small corrections are worth screening because early body errors matter more than the head alone. Start by checking whether fusion errors share a few predictable directions. A correction that reads raw values can recover information that binary signs discarded, but it makes the drafter a hybrid. Tiny storage does not guarantee tiny latency when each draft step launches extra work. Keep the candidate only if native acceptance and total throughput improve against Q4_0.
